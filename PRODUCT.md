# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineers, scientists, and technical procurement buyers in optics, photonics,
imaging, and laser markets, evaluating Edmund Optics as a supplier of optical
components, systems, and metrology equipment. They arrive with a specific
technical need (a market/application overview, a product category, a
knowledge-base article) and are assessing credibility and capability, not
browsing for entertainment. Purchase and specification decisions often
involve a team and a sales/engineering conversation, so content pages need to
build trust and route to the right next step (contact, catalog, resources).

## Product Purpose

Edmund Optics' EDS-powered marketing and content surface: landing pages,
market/application overview pages, and knowledge-base content that establish
technical credibility and move a technical buyer toward engaging sales or
browsing the product catalog. Success looks like a technical visitor
recognizing deep domain expertise within seconds and finding a clear path to
the next action (contact, resource download, related products).

## Brand Personality

Precise, authoritative, technical. Voice is confident through rigor rather
than hype — plain, specific, engineering-grade language over marketing
flourish. Emotional goal: the visitor should feel "these people understand
my problem at the engineering level."

## Anti-references

Generic corporate stock-photo B2B: bland stock imagery, vague icon grids,
interchangeable SaaS-template layouts that could belong to any company.
Also avoid overly flashy/consumer-style e-commerce motion or color that would

## Design Principles

- Engineering-grade credibility first — hierarchy and copy should read like
  it was written by people who understand the optics domain, not generic
  marketing copy.
- Show, don't tell — favor concrete specs, diagrams, and real product/data
  imagery over abstract claims.
- Calm confidence — restrained motion and color; let content density and
  precision carry the page rather than visual flourish.
- Clear next step — every content page should make the path to
  contact/catalog/resources obvious without being pushy.
- Respect the existing AEM EDS block system — new design should extend
  current blocks/tokens rather than fork a parallel visual system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline, matching this project's existing standard (AGENTS.md):
alt text on all images, proper heading hierarchy, keyboard operability,
sufficient contrast including text-over-image.
